Steps to follow:
1. Download the Consult to Labs B2C Leads sheet from the Acefone CDR.
2. Drop the downloaded sheet onto the "Data A" section of the site.
3. Copy the numbers in column B in the 'Consult Cart Leads' tab in B2C Leads sheet.
4. Paste those numbers into the "Data B" section of the site.
5. In the Dates section, update the current date. Leave other sections untouched.
6. Click on "Find unmatched rows"
7. Click on "Copy result".
8. Paste it on new rows in  'Consult Cart Leads' tab in the B2C Leads sheet.
9. Repeat all the steps for every iteration.
